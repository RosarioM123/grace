financials narrative

Strong revenue potential from just a quarter of users

Tested with more than five thousand on waitlist. We modeled only a quarter converting, the rest staying on community access. Even at that conservative share, the mix of standard and premium as access levels produces meaningful annual recurring without pressure to monetize everyone.

Growth is described qualitatively: year one establishes a launch cohort with higher share of community access, year two doubles active base as church partners activate, year three broadens to sixty thousand total with a heavier share moving into structured and personalized care. No dollar figures shown here, only the shape of the curve. Freemium stays majority, standard grows as habit, premium grows as outcomes.

Pricing was informed by direct survey, not by copying therapy marketplaces. Willingness maps to faith integrated value, not to generic telehealth.
